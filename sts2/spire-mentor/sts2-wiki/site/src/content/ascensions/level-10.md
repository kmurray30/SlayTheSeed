---
title: Double Boss
level: 10
description: Fight two bosses at the end of Act 3.
detail: At the end of the final act, you face two bosses instead of one. The primary boss is determined normally, and a second boss is randomly selected from the remaining boss pool. They are two separate fights on two separate map nodes — the second boss sits one row above the first and is reached by walking to it, so the final act is one floor longer than at lower ascensions.
